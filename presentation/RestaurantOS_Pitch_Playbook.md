# 🏆 RestaurantOS — In-Person Sales & Pitch Playbook
> **Confidential Field Sales Guide** | For pitching Dine-in Restaurant Owners, Café Chains, and Cloud Kitchen Operators.

---

## 🎯 1. Target Profile & Timing

### The Golden Window
- **Best Time:** **3:30 PM to 5:30 PM** (Monday through Thursday).
  - *Why:* Lunch rush ends by 3:00 PM. Dinner prep begins around 6:30 PM. The owner or managing partner is almost always sitting at a corner table doing accounts, vendor payments, or resting.
- **Worst Time (NEVER visit):** 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM and 8:00 PM – 10:30 PM. (Staff is slammed, kitchen is in rush, owner will be irritated).

### Who to Speak With
- Ask for: **The Owner**, **Managing Partner**, or **General Manager (GM)**.
- If captain/cashier says *"Owner is not here"*:
  - Ask: *"At what time does sir/ma'am usually come in to check the daily accounts?"* (Usually 4:30 PM or 10:30 PM). Get their name and visiting card.

## ⚡ 2. The 30-Second Doorstep Hook

When you walk in, the cashier or head captain will greet you. Don't sound like a generic credit card or insurance salesman. Use this specific script:

> *"Hi! Good afternoon. I'm [Your Name]. We work with restaurant owners in [Local Area, e.g., Indiranagar / Connaught Place / Bandra] to help stop cash leakages at the billing counter and save that 25% aggregator commission on dine-in guests.*  
>

When the owner sits down, open with:

> *"Sir, most owners I talk to tell me two big headaches keep them awake at night:*

>  
> *We built **RestaurantOS** specifically to fix these two problems forever. Can I show you a 3-minute live preview on this screen?"*

  > 2. *They don't have real-time WhatsApp instant theft alerts when cashiers cancel bills.*

  > *You don't have to cancel Petpooja today — run RestaurantOS for 14 days on our free pilot alongside your existing machine and see the difference yourself."*

### Objection 2: *"My waiters and captains aren't tech-savvy."*
- **Response:**  
  > *"If your waiters know how to use WhatsApp, they can use RestaurantOS. Punching an order takes just 3 taps. We also provide full in-person staff training on Day 1, and our interface is available in Hindi, English, and regional languages."*

### Objection 3: *"What if the internet drops during peak dinner rush?"*
- **Response:**  
  > *"RestaurantOS has offline local queueing. Your staff can continue taking orders and printing KOTs even if the broadband disconnects. The moment Wi-Fi or 4G returns, it synchronizes with the cloud automatically."*

### Objection 4: *"I don't want to buy expensive POS computers or terminals."*
- **Response:**  
  > *"Zero hardware investment required. RestaurantOS runs in any web browser. You can use an existing Android tablet, an iPad, a ₹6,000 Android phone, or the Windows laptop you already have at the counter."*

### Objection 5: *"Will it work with my existing thermal bill printer?"*
- **Response:**  
  > *"Yes, 100%. We support all standard 80mm and 58mm ESC/POS USB, Bluetooth, and LAN thermal receipt printers (Epson, TVS, NGX, Everycom)."*
